Genetic algorithm — the marks checklist

Q5 is mandatory, 20 marks, split Methodology 10 / Justification 10. The justification half is parts (d) and (e) — words, not arithmetic. Do not skip them.

  • (a) Table: chromosome, x, f(x), probability = f(x) / sum. Show the Total row.
  • (b) Crossover at bit k = swap everything from position k+1 onward.
  • (c) Mutation at bit position p = flip that one bit. Draw before and after.
  • (d) An offspring is accepted if its fitness beats the member it replaces — that is what raises the population's average fitness.
  • (e) Compare best-before with best-after and say plainly whether it improved. "No" is often the correct answer — a single GA iteration is not guaranteed to improve anything.

N-Queens fitness

  • Chromosome index = column, value = row.
  • Attack if same row, or |row diff| = |col diff| (diagonal).
  • Fitness = non-attacking pairs = n(n−1)/2 − attacks. For 5 queens the max is 10; for 8 queens, 28.
  • Compare each pair of columns exactly once.

CSP

  • Represent = variables, domains, constraints. Then draw the constraint graph: one node per variable, an edge for every binary constraint.
  • MRV (minimum remaining values) — assign the variable with the fewest legal values left. Ties broken by the degree heuristic (most constraints on unassigned variables).
  • Least-constraining value — among values, choose the one that rules out fewest options for the neighbours.
  • Forward checking — after each assignment, delete now-impossible values from unassigned neighbours' domains. Empty domain = backtrack immediately, which is the whole point: it fails earlier than plain backtracking.

Bayesian networks

  • Joint = product over nodes of P(node | its parents). Write that product before plugging numbers in.
  • Any variable not in the query gets summed out.
  • Conditional = joint / marginal. Compute the marginal once and reuse it.
  • Check: P(+q | e) + P(−q | e) = 1. State it — free marks.
  • Bayes' rule: P(A|B) = P(B|A) P(A) / P(B).

Neural network (Fall 2024-25 Q2)

Forward pass, then total error. The paper's setup: ReLU f(x) = max(0, x) on the hidden layer, sigmoid f(x) = 1/(1 + e^−x) on the output layer. Hidden nodes share one bias; output nodes have no bias.

  1. For each hidden node: net = sum(w × input) + bias, then apply ReLU (negative becomes 0).
  2. For each output node: net = sum(w × hidden), no bias, then apply sigmoid.
  3. Total error E = sum of ½(target − output)² over the output nodes.

Read the weights straight off your figure and keep 3-4 decimals throughout.

Alpha-beta

Also on the suggestion sheet, and mechanically identical to the midterm question: pass α/β down to children, MAX raises α, MIN lowers β, prune the remaining children the moment α ≥ β (equality counts). Left to right.
